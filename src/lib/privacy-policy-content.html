Privacy Policy

Radiant Guard Services Pvt. Ltd. ("Radiant Guard", "we", "us") respects the privacy of every person whose information we handle. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and what rights you have in relation to it. It applies to our mobile applications, our workforce and administration platform, our websites, and any other service that links to this policy.

This policy is issued under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") of India and the rules made under it as notified from time to time. For the purposes of that law, Radiant Guard Services Pvt. Ltd. is the Data Fiduciary. Where we process data on behalf of a client at the client's instruction, the client is the Data Fiduciary and we act as a Data Processor.

Effective date: 12 September 2026. Version 1.0.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the personal data of:

  • Our employees, security officers, supervisors, field officers, reliefers and other personnel, current and former;
  • Candidates and applicants who apply or are onboarded through us;
  • Personnel, contacts and representatives of our clients and contract sites;
  • Vendors, drivers, suppliers and their representatives; and
  • Visitors to our websites and applications.

The platform is a business and workforce tool. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 years of age.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you or your employer give us

  • Identity details: full name, date of birth, gender, photograph, signature, marital status, birthplace, and where you choose to provide it, religion, caste category and ex-service status;
  • Contact details: mobile number, alternate mobile number, email address, permanent and present addresses, police station details, and emergency contact name, relation and number;
  • Statutory identity documents: Aadhaar number and Aadhaar image, PAN number and PAN image, ESIC card, and other identification proofs you submit;
  • Financial details for salary and reimbursement: bank account number, account holder name, bank name, branch, IFSC code and account type;
  • Employment and qualification details: designation, department, unit or site, shift, joining and departure dates, reports-to, education, experience, references, languages, nominations and self-declared physical health information;
  • Documents you upload: appointment and agreement papers, identity proofs, certificates, photographs, signed letters and acknowledgements; and
  • Content of communications you send us: messages, complaints, support requests and feedback.

2.2 Information from verification services

To verify identity and complete onboarding we use trusted verification services, including DigiLocker (operated by UIDAI) accessed through our verification partner SurePass, and background verification providers. From these services we may receive verified identity attributes such as name, date of birth, address, masked or full Aadhaar as returned by the service, PAN status, and bank account name confirmation, together with the verification result.

2.3 Information collected automatically through the applications

  • Attendance data: check-in and check-out times, the site or unit you punched against, and the method used;
  • Location data: GPS coordinates and accuracy captured at the moment of attendance, and, where enabled for your role, periodic location points and visit check-in and check-out coordinates used to confirm site presence and patrol coverage;
  • Face verification results: a yes or no record confirming that a camera capture matched your registered photograph at the time of a punch. Where enabled, the capture itself is processed for that check;
  • Device and connection data: device model, operating system, app version, push notification token, battery state, network type, IP address and language preference;
  • Usage and log data: pages and features used, actions performed, timestamps, and the IP address and phone number associated with each sign-in, recorded so that changes can be audited; and
  • Camera and photo access: used only when you or your supervisor take a picture for attendance, an incident report, a stock issue, a vehicle entry or a client sign-off.

We do not store your fingerprint or facial biometric template. Device biometric unlock such as Face ID is handled entirely by your phone's operating system; the app only receives an "allow" or "deny" result and never sees the biometric itself.

2.4 Information from clients and third parties

Where you work at a client site, the client may give us your name, photograph, access-card or badge details, site instructions, incident reports and complaints about your attendance or conduct, which we then hold alongside your service record.

3. How we use your information

  • To recruit, onboard, verify and deploy you, and to issue appointment letters, postings and duty orders;
  • To run payroll and reimbursements accurately, including salary, extra duty, allowances, deductions, and advances;
  • To calculate and deposit statutory contributions and filings on your behalf, including Provident Fund, ESIC, Professional Tax, Labour Welfare Fund and income-tax deduction at source;
  • To record and verify attendance, leave, overtime or extra duty, and to settle disputes about them;
  • To carry out background verification and periodic re-verification required by clients or by law;
  • To send you operational alerts, task assignments, shift and roster changes, approvals, and pay-related notifications;
  • To maintain safety and security of people and property at the sites where we operate, and to respond to incidents and emergencies;
  • To provide, secure and improve our services, including troubleshooting, abuse monitoring and audit trails;
  • To meet contractual obligations to our clients, and to defend or enforce legal rights; and
  • For any other purpose you consent to, or that is otherwise permitted by law.

We process personal data for a lawful purpose on the basis of your consent, of the employment or service relationship, of a legal or contractual obligation, or of a legitimate purpose that does not unreasonably affect your interests. Where processing depends on consent, you may withdraw it at any time as described in section 9.

4. Sensitive personal data

Some of the information described above, such as Aadhaar and PAN numbers, bank and financial details, photographs and face-verification records, health information, and precise location, is treated as sensitive personal data. We collect it only where it is necessary for the purposes set out above, we show it only to people who need it for their work, and we prefer masked or partial forms wherever the purpose allows (for example, DigiLocker normally returns a masked Aadhaar and we retain that masked form where the full number is not required). Please do not send Aadhaar, PAN, bank or card details to us over email, WhatsApp or SMS; share them only inside the onboarding screen or as a document upload.

5. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising. We share it only in the ways below.

  • Clients and contract sites: attendance sheets, invoices, deployment and incident records, and site-entry information, so that service delivery can be verified and billed;
  • Statutory authorities and regulators: EPFO, ESIC, the Labour and Factories departments, the GST and Income Tax departments, the police and courts, and any other body entitled to require it;
  • Service providers who work for us under contract, listed in the annexure below;
  • Banks, insurers and payment channels for salary, ESIC and insurance purposes;
  • Our auditors, legal advisers and, in a reorganisation, a successor business, subject to confidentiality; and
  • Any other person, with your consent, or where we are required by law or need to protect a vital interest such as someone's safety.

Every provider listed in the annexure is engaged under written terms that restrict how it may use the data and require appropriate security. Access inside our own organisation follows role-based permissions: a user sees only the units and records their role and scope allow, and every meaningful change is written to an internal audit log.

6. Where your data is processed and stored

Your records are hosted on secure cloud infrastructure with our platform provider, and may be processed or stored in data centres both inside and outside India, including the United States and Singapore, depending on the service. Where data is transferred outside India we require the provider to maintain a level of protection consistent with this policy and with applicable Indian law. Some features also load resources such as fonts and map tiles from third-party servers, which may see your IP address.

7. How long we keep your information

We keep personal data only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy, and in any event for the periods required by law. In practice:

  • Employment, payroll and statutory records are retained for at least the periods required under the EPF and MP Act, the ESI Act, the Companies Act, 2013, the Income Tax Act and the GST Act, which in several cases means eight years or more after the relevant year;
  • Attendance, location and audit records are retained while they are needed for verification, dispute resolution and payroll, and are then archived or deleted;
  • Device push tokens are removed when you uninstall the app, stop using the account, or when the notification service reports the device as no longer registered; and
  • For a candidate whose application is unsuccessful, onboarding data is kept for a limited period so that we can respond to a query or re-approach you, and is then deleted or anonymised.

Where the law permits no deletion, we isolate and protect the record instead of using it.

8. How we protect your information

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest on the hosting platform;
  • Sign-in protected by one-time passwords sent to your registered mobile number, and optional device biometric unlock that never leaves your phone;
  • Role-based access control and data scoping so that supervisors and managers see only their own people and sites;
  • Network-level sign-in controls, including the ability to restrict sign-ins to approved IP ranges;
  • Central audit logging of sign-ins and of changes to sensitive records such as contracts, wages and invoices;
  • Least-privilege credentials and secrets stored in a managed vault rather than in code; and
  • Review of providers' security, and a commitment to notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected people in the event of a personal data breach, as required by the DPDP Act.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please keep your phone, your device unlock PIN and your OTP confidential, and tell us immediately if your phone is lost or you suspect that your account has been used without your permission.

9. Your rights and how to exercise them

Subject to the DPDP Act and to any exemption that applies to your employer, you may:

  1. Ask for a summary of the personal data we process about you, and of who received it;
  2. Ask us to correct, complete or update inaccurate or misleading personal data;
  3. Ask us to erase personal data, where we are no longer required by law to retain it;
  4. Withdraw consent previously given, in which case we will stop the processing that relied on it, though we may continue to process what the law or the contract requires;
  5. Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity; and
  6. Lodge a grievance and receive a resolution.

Requests are free of charge. We may ask you to prove your identity before responding, and we will not normally remove information we are legally obliged to keep. We aim to respond within thirty days.

The DPDP Act also places duties on data principals: to give only lawful and genuine consent, to provide accurate information, to notify us of incorrect responses, and not to make frivolous or vexatious complaints.

10. Grievance redressal

For any privacy question, request or complaint, write to the Grievance Officer at the addresses in section 12. If we do not resolve your grievance in the time stated, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective date" and "Version" above and, where the change materially affects you, notify you in the app, by SMS or by email before the change takes effect. The previous version remains available on request.
