- Contract PF/ESI lines are per-line formulas; optional keys `esiLimitOn` + `esiLimitBasis` ("payrate"|"earnings") decide ESI eligibility, `roundMode` ("up"|"nearest") rounds, and an uncapped EPF formula is never clipped — matches Pages Payroll settings without per-client code.
- Per-guard allowances live in `additions` (site-scoped `unit_id`, `repeat_monthly`/`end_date`, `prorate_by_days`, `counts_for_pf`/`counts_for_esi`); payroll and invoice apply them only via `applyEmployeeAdditions` (ESI-eligible additions also feed formula-based ESI lines, rounded once at the end) — person-specific pay never edits the shared contract rate.
- Professional Tax is one monthly slab per person via `ptForSite` (`pt-cross-site.ts`): until another site has charged PT, the site with most month duties (`ptSpread`, tie → lowest unit id) charges the slab on estimated combined gross and the rest charge 0; once a processed site charged PT (`payroll_run_snapshots`), others pay slab(combined) − PT already charged, and Process re-checks — mirrors Pages so multi-site guards are taxed once even before processing.
- PT and LWF use `units.pt_state` (work state, mirrors Pages' PT state) when set, else billing state/pincode — billing state is only the invoice address (often head office). LWF resolves pincode → state, falling back to the site billing state when the pincode is blank or outside every `pincode_ranges` row; Extra-Duty-only lines drop EPF/ESI but keep the contract PT row — matches Pages, and sites without a mapped pincode still get LWF.
- Invoices bill the saved `contract_resources` amounts, so any import or bulk write must store formula lines already evaluated exactly as the contract editor does on Save (`computeBenefitAmount`); otherwise pressing Save silently changes the billing rate.
