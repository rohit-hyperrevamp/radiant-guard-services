# Modern notification and mobile drawer treatment

## Changes
- Redesign the phone notification drawer with a cleaner header, clearer unread state, grouped spacing, and a polished bottom-sheet shape.
- Map notification categories to meaningful colored icons so attendance, payroll, invoices, employees, contracts, inventory, organizations, field activity, and general alerts are immediately recognizable.
- Keep notification actions compact and accessible, including sound, mark-all-read, close, and Notification Center.
- Match the More expansion to the notification drawer’s dimmed blurred background, motion, depth, rounded treatment, and spacing while retaining the black dock.
- Standardize shared dialog, sheet, drawer, and confirmation overlays to use the same blur strength and fade behavior throughout mobile forms and slide-up experiences.

## Validation
- Run the project TypeScript check.
- Inspect the phone layout at the current viewport and confirm notification and More panels do not overlap the dock or safe area.
